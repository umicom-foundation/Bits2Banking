Pricing Financial Instruments from Cash Flows

Use fictional payments to value a deposit, a bond, a forward and a one-step option. Explain what changes when a model input moves.

Before you start

Start after fractions, percentages and the functions lesson. A calculator is enough. Derivatives are introduced before the sensitivity lesson, and earlier exercises can be done separately.

Lessons

  1. Put payments on a timeline and discount them

    Read a dated cash flow, distinguish present and future value, and calculate a one-period discount.

  2. Put a price on a bond's dated promises

    List each coupon and face-value payment, discount the payments, and compare the results at two rates.

  3. Measure a bond price's rate sensitivity

    Estimate a local price response to a small change in the assumed rate and distinguish a percentage point from a percent.

  4. Distinguish a forward delivery price from its value

    Read a forward payoff and tell apart the agreed delivery price and the value of an existing contract.

  5. Price a call option in a two-outcome model

    Calculate a call payoff in each outcome and discount the weighted result in a one-step model.
