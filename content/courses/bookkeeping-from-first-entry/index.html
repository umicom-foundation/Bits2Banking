Bookkeeping from Your First Entry

Follow the workshop's receipts and payments from the source document to a journal, ledger, trial balance and reconciliation.

Before you start

Counting and signed cents are enough to start. You can use paper throughout. The workshop and every transaction are fictional.

Lessons

  1. Trace a receipt into accounts

    Distinguish a source document, a transaction and an account, then check the accounting equation.

  2. Record both sides of a transaction

    Post one debit and one credit to a journal, then summarise the movements in a ledger.

  3. Prepare a trial balance and find its limits

    Prepare a trial balance from ledger balances, and explain one error that balanced columns cannot reveal.

  4. Reconcile the cash book with a statement

    Compare two dated cash records and distinguish timing differences from entries that need review.
