Practical progression

Begin by identifying what a cryptographic commitment guarantees. Next, distinguish independently trusted ledger checkpoints from participant-supplied proofs. Then study federated learning, signed evidence and governance with negative tests. The final exercises combine checked arithmetic and controlled synthetic input.

Merkle inclusion, message authentication, permissioned authorisation, distributed consensus and useful neural learning are distinct capabilities. The native Framework exercises implement only the explicitly described local primitives. No public cryptocurrency or production blockchain is implied.
