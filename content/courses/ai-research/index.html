  • 006 — Choose RAG, SFT, preference learning and verifiable rewards
  • 007 — Design contamination-resistant and cost-aware evaluations
  • 008 — Low-level AI optimisation on x86, ARM and RISC-V

Study approach

Begin with the concept and worked example, run the native C23 tests or learner laboratory, deliberately trigger a rejection, and record what has not been established. A hash is not a quality score; a numerical average is not a trained model; a blockchain is not itself distributed gradient descent.
