Course exercises

For every lesson, reproduce the worked example, uncover the answer only after attempting the task, and explain whether the result is proof of inclusion, an authorisation decision, a consensus rule or an experimental quality observation. These evidence categories are not interchangeable.

The C23 Merkle library is an educational Framework capability; blockchain consensus, production key management, smart contracts and real multi-institution deployments are not implemented by it.
