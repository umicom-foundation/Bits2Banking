BITS TO BANKING · FOUNDATION PATHWAY

Maths for AI — From the Beginning

Numbers → vectors → matrices → probability → gradients → rotary positions → attention.

For students with any educational or professional background. No calculus, programming, financial markets or AI knowledge is required to begin.

How to study this course

You can read every unit in order or take a short detour when a native AI lesson becomes difficult. Every unit explains its formulas in words, substitutes real numbers, includes a diagram, and ends with a worked answer you can reveal. The examples use Umicom software—notes, customer queries, training models and simulated finance—not meaningless sample phrases.

  1. 01Numbers, fractions and percentagesFractions, percentages and numerical comparisons
  2. 02Variables, expressions and functionsWhat letters mean and how to follow a function
  3. 03Vectors, coordinates and dot productsOrdered numbers and similarity-style calculations
  4. 04Matrices, shapes and multiplicationTables, shapes, matrix products and transpose
  5. 05Probability, softmax and learning lossFrom scores to probabilities and training loss
  6. 06Derivatives, gradients and the chain ruleWhy gradients allow a model to learn
  7. 07Angles, sine, cosine and rotary positionTurn two coordinates; understand RoPE
  8. 08Attention, token positions and KV-cache workCompute tiny attention and examine caching
  9. 09Reading mathematical formulas in native C23Understand code, tolerances and notation
  10. 10Gradient descent and learning ratesMake and check one numerical improvement at a time
  11. 11Matrix weights and a complete training stepWork through every multiplication and gradient
  12. 12Finite differences and numerical agreementCheck gradients independently and choose tolerances
  13. 13From attention scores to a weighted answerLearn how two tokens produce one meaningful weighted result, with every symbol explained.
  14. 14Verify gradients independentlyUse two nearby loss measurements to check an automatic gradient and one SGD weight update.
  15. 15Repeatability, precision and trustworthy evidenceUnderstand exact repeats, acceptable numerical differences, memory accounting and honest limits.
