Experimental boundaries

The accompanying C23 Framework component validates portable binary representations and composes existing federated-job arithmetic. The local experiment exchanges synthetic results between separate operating-system processes using temporary files. It does not yet train a real model over a network, authenticate peers or implement blockchain consensus. Each lesson provides a worked example, self-check and research source.
