# Mathematics for AI — Unit 19: Evidence and Numerical Tolerances

**Author:** Sammy Hegab | Umicom Foundation | UK English

## Purpose
Build from familiar arithmetic before introducing notation or a computer programme.

## Worked example
measured=10.004, reference=10.000, absolute error=0.004.

## Symbols
`|x|` means absolute value; `≤` means less than or equal to; `atol` and `rtol` are absolute and relative tolerances.

A combined tolerance uses `|a-b| ≤ atol + rtol×|b|`. The right-hand expression is a permitted error, not an amount to subtract from the measured result.

## Exercise
Work through a second example using different numerical values. Explain each stage aloud, without copying the formula blindly.
