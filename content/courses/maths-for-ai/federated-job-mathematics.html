How these lessons relate to Umicom

These public lessons teach distributed-training jobs, arithmetic, research controls and why the existence of a SHA-256 digest or a ledger does not prove useful neural learning. The Framework C23 implementation is a bounded local coordinator, not a permissionless network. Finish every exercise before reading its answer and compare results to the declared assumptions.
