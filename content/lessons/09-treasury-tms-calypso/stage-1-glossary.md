# Stage 1 glossary — words you will meet in treasury

A useful financial glossary should answer questions, not merely repeat unfamiliar jargon. You can come back to this page whenever a word is new.

| Word | Plain-English meaning | Example from the course |
|---|---|---|
| Account | A record of amounts held or owed in a defined relationship | The kitchen's GBP bank account |
| Available funds | Money that can actually be used under the account's conditions | A balance less a hold or restriction |
| Book | An organisational grouping for recorded trading activity | The fictional `LONDON_FX` desk book |
| Cashflow | Money expected to move on a specified date | A £6,000 supplier payment on Tuesday |
| Cash ladder | A forecast arranged by payment and receipt dates | Monday to Friday funding table |
| Confirmation | A communication checking or recording agreed terms | A counterparty confirms an FX trade |
| Counterparty | The other party to a financial agreement | The bank facing the company in an FX forward |
| Currency | The monetary unit used for an amount | GBP, USD or EUR |
| Deposit | Money placed with a bank or other institution under agreed terms | A £10,000 90-day deposit |
| Event | A notice that something happened in a system | A trade reaching an authorised status |
| FX forward | An agreement to exchange currencies on a future date at agreed terms | Buy USD 125,000, sell GBP 100,000 |
| Funding | Obtaining money to meet financial commitments | Arranging cash for Tuesday's supplier |
| Idempotency | Preventing repeated processing from creating repeated business effects | Do not pay an invoice twice after a retry |
| Legal entity | A company or other recognised legal organisation involved in the trade | Fictional Bank A or Corporate B |
| Liquidity | Being able to pay obligations when due | Having enough cash on Tuesday |
| Maturity | The agreed end date of a financial contract | Return of deposit principal and interest |
| Middle office | A set of independent valuation and control responsibilities | Checking limits and trade accuracy |
| Principal | Original amount deposited or borrowed | £10,000 placed on deposit |
| Product | A type of financial instrument or contract | A bond or FX forward |
| Reconciliation | Comparing records and explaining differences | TMS payment versus bank statement |
| Settlement | Completion of cash or securities obligations under applicable rules | Both currency legs reach settled state |
| TMS | Treasury Management System, software for controlled treasury activities | Cash forecasts, contracts, controls and reports |
| Trade | A specific financial transaction between parties | The company's own FX forward contract |
| Transfer | An operational obligation to move cash or securities | Pay GBP 100,000 on the forward date |
| Treasury | The function planning funding, liquidity and financial risks | Ensuring Tuesday's supplier can be paid |
| Workflow | Rules and actions that control how an item changes status | NEW → VERIFIED → AUTHORISED |

The definitions are introductory. Later stages explore contract-specific, accounting and regulatory meanings in greater detail.
