# Introduction to Distributed Learning

[Lesson library](../index.md) · [Learning sequence](../../start/learning-sequence.md) · [Browser course](../../courses/distributed-learning/index.html)

First learn what a model, a training step and a checkpoint are in Building an LLM from Scratch. A worker is a computer performing one assigned part of a larger task. This course then studies how several workers communicate and how their results are checked.
