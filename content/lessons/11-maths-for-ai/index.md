# Mathematics for AI — Written Lessons

**Bits2Banking · Umicom Foundation · Sammy Hegab**

This directory is the designated location for the **editable Markdown manuscripts** of the Mathematics for AI course. The [illustrated HTML course](../../courses/maths-for-ai/index.html) remains available in its original location. Formulas are explained in familiar language before formal notation.
