## Suggested learning route

Read Units [5](05-probability-softmax-and-loss.md), [10](10-gradient-descent-and-learning-rate.md), [21](21-cross-entropy-for-native-fine-tuning.md) and [25](25-loss-masks-and-next-token-alignment.md) first if the notation is unfamiliar. Then move through Units 29–32 in order and revisit the [native supervised-epoch lesson](../10-native-ai/train-with-deterministic-epochs.md).

Each calculation uses entirely fictional, synthetic numbers. The lessons do not replace Framework implementation, real test evidence or independently approved training-data policies.
