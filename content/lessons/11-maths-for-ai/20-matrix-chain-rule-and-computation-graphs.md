# Mathematics for AI — Unit 20: Chain Rule and Computation Graphs

**Author:** Sammy Hegab | Umicom Foundation | UK English

## Purpose
Build from familiar arithmetic before introducing notation or a computer programme.

## Worked example
w=3, u=2w=6, L=u²=36, derivative dL/dw=24.

## Symbols
`w` means a weight, `u` means an intermediate value, `L` means loss and `dL/dw` describes how quickly loss changes when the weight changes. The chain rule multiplies the rates of change along the pathway.

## Exercise
Work through a second example using different numerical values. Explain each stage aloud, without copying the formula blindly.
