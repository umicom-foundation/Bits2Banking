The first [mathematics course](index.md) starts with arithmetic and grows towards matrices, derivatives and numerical evidence. These supplementary chapters practise the calculations needed to understand a **bounded, trainable stacked decoder**. The original illustrated HTML course remains available through the mathematics index.

4. **[Combine cross-entropy for selected answer tokens](50-combine-selected-token-cross-entropies.md)**.
5. **[Understand deterministic training permutations](51-understand-deterministic-training-permutations.md)**.
6. **[Measure a portable stacked-model checkpoint](52-measure-portable-stacked-checkpoint-sizes.md)**.
7. **[Compare training loss with held-out loss](53-compare-training-and-heldout-loss.md)**.
8. **[Estimate full-context generation work across layers](54-estimate-full-context-generation-work.md)**.

Use this alongside the [native multi-layer course](../10-native-ai/stacked-transformer-learning-path.md). Worked examples use invented numbers for teaching; they are not real banking values or model-performance measurements.
