## How to study

Read one lesson, predict the example result, then attempt the exercise before opening its explained answer. Save optional practice programs in a folder you choose. Expected outputs describe what to check; they are not a record that a command has run on your computer.

## Planned continuation

Next modules: dated cashflow schedules and calendars; coupon bonds and accrued interest; discount curves and interpolation; FX forwards and quote conventions; swaps; option payoffs before pricing models; sensitivities; calibration; model limitations; C interfaces and Java adapters; reproducible valuation reports.
