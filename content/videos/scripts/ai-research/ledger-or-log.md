# Video lesson script — Do we need blockchain to verify learning?

**Umicom Foundation · Written for learners · Sammy Hegab**

## Scene 1 — Narration and on-screen teaching

Hash chains can preserve ordered tamper evidence; cryptographic signatures bind statements to keys.

## Scene 2 — Narration and on-screen teaching

A MAC is a shared-secret channel assurance, not an independently checkable public receipt.

## Scene 3 — Narration and on-screen teaching

A blockchain adds multi-administrator consensus and economic costs, but cannot certify that a gradient improves a model.

## Scene 4 — Narration and on-screen teaching

Record a falsifiable hypothesis comparing a signed log and a consensus ledger under the same threat model.

## Visual and accessibility description

Use labelled text and an accessible transcript. Introduce each diagram through its underlying numbers before animation. Keep private learner data and credentials out of the illustrative screens.
