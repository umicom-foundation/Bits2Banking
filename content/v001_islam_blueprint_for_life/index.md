# Volume 1 — Islam: Blueprint for Life

![Islam: Blueprint for Life book cover](../assets/books/islam/islam_bookcover.png)

Islam is presented here as a complete way of life: faith, worship, character, responsibility, honest dealings, family life and service to others. The volume explores the purpose of life, the history of money, banking, financial justice and the role technology can play in the service of society.

The chapters are part of the original *Bits to Banking* book collection, by Sammy Hegab and the Umicom Foundation. They are available alongside the computing, programming and financial-systems courses.

## From faith to useful technology

The Islamic emphasis on seeking beneficial knowledge, fulfilling trusts, fairness and helping others informs the Umicom Foundation's educational philosophy. See [Faith, service and technology](faith-service-and-technology.md) for connections to secure software, clear financial records, responsible engineering and open education.

You can then follow [computer fundamentals](../lessons/01-computers/index.md), [software engineering](../lessons/05-engineering/index.md) and [financial systems](../lessons/07-practical-domains/index.md). Each route can be read independently.
