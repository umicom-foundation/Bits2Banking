# Draft a transaction and receipt card

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional transfer T37 moves 80 units from an account with 210 to an account with 90. The receiver uses correlation C4 for its evidence.

1. Write the before and after account rows and assign T37 to the database change and its receipt.
2. Draw the transaction around both changes and the audit row. Add C4 beside the outward response as a correlation value.

**Check your result.** The committed balances are 130 and 170 and still sum to 300. The transaction records T37 once and the response carries C4.
