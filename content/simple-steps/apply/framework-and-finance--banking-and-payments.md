# Make a customer balance view with two accounts

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A synthetic customer C3 holds two GBP accounts with ledger amounts of 2,400 and 600 minor units. The first has a 400-unit active reservation, and the second has none.

1. Write one account row per identity. Show both ledger and available amounts after applying the reservation.
2. Compute two separate customer totals for GBP, using the ledger column for one and the available column for the other.

**Check your result.** The account rows show availability of 2,000 and 600. The view shows GBP ledger total 3,000 and available total 2,600.
