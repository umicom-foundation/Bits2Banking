# Build a small job summary with named states

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A local desk report receives the job states queued, ready, and queued, represented as three enum entries.

1. Use the Enum and Select Case lesson to give queued and ready readable labels. Visit the input entries once and count both states.
2. Print both totals with their corresponding labels. Add an explicit branch for values outside the two states in the scratch routine.

**Check your result.** The sample report shows Queued 2 and Ready 1 with no unknown job.
