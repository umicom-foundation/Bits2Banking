# Make a three-account local exception report

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Account A matches at 120 cents, B has 90 versus 95 cents and C appears on only one side at 150 cents.

1. Enter those accounts in the course’s two-sided sample tables. Run or trace the comparison query.
2. Record expected and actual amounts and list C’s missing side separately from B’s difference.

**Check your result.** A matches, B differs by 5 cents, and C has a missing counterpart rather than a calculated zero.
