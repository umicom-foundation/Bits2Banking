# Produce a report with explicit absent data

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

One fictitious row has amount 42.70 and a present zero adjustment. A second amount is 15.00 with an absent adjustment.

1. Use the exact projection and its indicator from the course’s local exercise. Write both amounts as whole cents.
2. Produce a two-row practice report that separately labels the two adjustment states.

**Check your result.** The amounts are 4,270 and 1,500 cents. The adjustments appear as zero and absent respectively.
