# Connect a local route to a read-only list view

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A training catalogue has two fixed rows called Grind coffee and Heat water.

1. Map GET /checklist to a course controller method. Supply both rows as a view value from that method.
2. Render the rows as list items in the view and open /checklist on the local server.

**Check your result.** The page lists Grind coffee followed by Heat water under its Checklist heading.
