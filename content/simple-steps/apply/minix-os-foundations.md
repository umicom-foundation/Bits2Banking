# Prepare a restricted guest evidence sheet

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Your disposable MINIX guest produces a status line for an ordinary local service request.

1. Record the ISO identity and one server involved in that request. Use the course diagram to sketch the request and reply ownership.
2. Capture or trace the corresponding result. Put the expected response and observation in separate columns.

**Check your result.** The sheet links one left-to-right request and its matching right-to-left reply to a documented guest identity.
