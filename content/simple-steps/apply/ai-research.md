# Prepare an extraction register for a classroom dataset

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Your tiny training sample contains a one-minute video you made and an outside PDF whose reuse permission is unknown.

1. Create a two-row manifest with source name, ownership or permission, extracted segment identifier and admission decision. Use your own clip's segment from 00:10 to 00:20.
2. Add a short transcript from that segment to the admitted set. Leave the PDF as a pending entry and record what evidence could change its decision.

**Check your result.** The admitted set contains one transcript segment linked to the clip and its start and end times. The PDF is pending with no material copied to the set.
