# Build a maturity report for two currencies

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A worksheet contains an EUR receipt of 400 cents and a USD payment of 300 cents, both due Friday. The report uses original currencies.

1. Create one maturity entry per obligation with date, currency, amount and direction.
2. Group by currency inside the Friday section and show receipts and payments in separate rows.

**Check your result.** The Friday report shows EUR receivable 400 cents and USD payable 300 cents on distinct rows.
