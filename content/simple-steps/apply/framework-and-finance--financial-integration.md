# Prepare an offline payment report with a mapping trail

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

An invented payment source supplies amount text 4.70, currency GBP and reference V18. The independent practice statement records V18 for 470 pence.

1. Write the source-to-canonical mapping and store the amount with its unit. Record the independent statement as a second observation.
2. Create a one-row report showing both references, amounts and the outcome of comparing them.

**Check your result.** The canonical amount is GBP 470 minor units. Both observations agree on V18 and amount, and the report marks the fictional movement reconciled.
