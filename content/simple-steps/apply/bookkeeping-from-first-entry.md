# Create a journal and ledger for a new workshop

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional workshop starts with zero accounts, receives owner funding of £60 and borrows £40. It later purchases £25 of equipment with cash.

1. Write one balanced journal entry for each of the three events, using the Cash, Owner Capital, Loan Payable and Equipment accounts.
2. Group the posted lines by account. Check the resulting assets against liabilities plus equity.

**Check your result.** Cash is £75 and equipment is £25. Assets of £100 equal £40 of liabilities plus £60 of equity. Journal debits and credits each total £125.
