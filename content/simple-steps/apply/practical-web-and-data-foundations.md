# Make a short local reconciliation sheet

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice statement has posted rows of 480 and −120 cents and a pending row of 200 cents.

1. Enter all three in the documented local table and keep the states visible before summing.
2. Create a posted-total row and compare it to the worksheet calculation. Keep the pending amount visible separately.

**Check your result.** The posted total is 360 cents and one pending row displays 200 cents.
