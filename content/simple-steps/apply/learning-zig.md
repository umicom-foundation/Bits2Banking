# Build a small conversion with a refusal result

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A toy appliance accepts times from 1 to 15 minutes and needs whole seconds.

1. Use the course’s typed result and explicit-error pattern. Write the accepted range beside the conversion formula.
2. Run or trace 7 minutes and then 16 minutes. Save the two observations in a short report.

**Check your result.** 7 gives 420 seconds. The 16-minute input follows the explicit refusal path.
