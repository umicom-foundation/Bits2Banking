# Build a one-day list of cash needs

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two practice payments are due on Thursday. Account A owes 200 cents and account B owes 450 cents in the same currency.

1. Make a funding worksheet with one row per account and show both due amounts.
2. Total the amounts, then use the existing balances of A 100 cents and B 450 cents to find any shortfall by account.

**Check your result.** The due total is 650 cents. A is short by 100 cents and B has no shortfall.
