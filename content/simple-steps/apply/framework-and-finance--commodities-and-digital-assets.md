# Make a receiving card with units and delivery terms

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A mock order asks for 2.4 tonnes of grade G1 at Gate A on 8 June. The fictional delivery ticket reads 2,400 kilograms, grade G1, Gate A, 8 June.

1. Convert the ordered quantity to kilograms and record the original unit alongside the conversion.
2. Create a one-row check for mass, grade, place and date. Copy both references into the receiving card.

**Check your result.** The quantities match at 2,400 kilograms. Grade G1, Gate A and 8 June match too. The card keeps the original order and ticket identities.
