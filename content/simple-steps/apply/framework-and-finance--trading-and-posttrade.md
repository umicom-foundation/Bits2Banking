# Keep a report after a practice broker session closes

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A local simulated broker offers quotes of 19 practice cents for Cedar and 24 for Pine. A report must remain readable after the session releases its result list.

1. Read the two borrowed quotes and copy their symbol and price into a report-owned collection during the session.
2. Close the practice session and write the report using the collection owned by the report.

**Check your result.** The closed-session report still lists Cedar at 19 practice cents and Pine at 24.
