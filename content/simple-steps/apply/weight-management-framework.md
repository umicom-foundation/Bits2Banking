# Prepare a simple reviewed-entry record

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional log has one reviewed entry at 73,450 grams and one separate draft of 73,600 grams.

1. Use the course’s record identities to distinguish the stored revision and draft. Write both with consistent units.
2. Trace accepting the draft and revise the report while preserving the earlier observation.

**Check your result.** The report changes from 73,450 to 73,600 grams only after the new draft receives its own accepted revision.
