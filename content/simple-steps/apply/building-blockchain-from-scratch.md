# Specify an eight-byte teaching record

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A local classroom ledger wants a byte diagram for sequence 9 and amount 300 using its unsigned two-field file format.

1. Draw eight positions. Assign bytes 0–3 to the little-endian sequence and bytes 4–7 to the little-endian amount.
2. Encode the two field values in hex. Add the field names, permitted width and unit to the diagram.

**Check your result.** The byte diagram reads 09 00 00 00 2C 01 00 00. Its amount is 300 whole practice units.
