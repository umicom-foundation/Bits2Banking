# Make a signed cash-flow card

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional service agreement asks the holder to pay £30 today and receive £50 at day 365. The workshop assumes a zero discount rate.

1. Write a two-row card showing viewpoint, dates, currency and amounts with signs. Use Framework's discount-factor lesson to calculate the factor at each date.
2. Calculate the present value from each signed flow. Repeat the arithmetic from the other party's viewpoint on a separate row.

**Check your result.** The factors are both one. The holder has -£30 and +£50 for a net present value of £20. The other party has a net of -£20.
