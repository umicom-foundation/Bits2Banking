# Create a two-line purchase entry with evidence

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional business buys a £72 printer with cash. Its receipt is P19 and lists the same amount.

1. Draft the two journal lines with P19 and the account names. Use the equipment-and-cash rule in Read a balanced journal.
2. Write a brief lineage row from receipt P19 to the journal and then to the equipment report.

**Check your result.** The journal debits Equipment and credits Cash by £72 each. The equipment report can trace that movement back to the journal and P19.
