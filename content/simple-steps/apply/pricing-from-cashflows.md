# Prepare a two-payment classroom valuation

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A hypothetical training instrument pays £11 after one period and £121 after two periods. Use a 10 percent training rate.

1. Draw a two-row timeline and calculate the present value of each payment. Label the discount factor you used.
2. Add the two values and verify each by growing it back to its own payment date.

**Check your result.** The first value is £10 and the second is £100. The classroom total is £110.
