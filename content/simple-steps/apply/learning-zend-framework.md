# Prepare a printable venue notice

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A local announcement has the title `Community <Hall>` and two meeting times.

1. Pass the title and times through the data interface of the course’s local template. Keep the title visibly labelled.
2. Open and print-preview the result. Inspect one input title and the corresponding visual heading in your notes.

**Check your result.** The notice has one clearly titled block, two times and literal angle brackets in the rendered title.
