# Add a reversal to a practice ledger

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A paper ledger gives A 12 credits and B zero. A recorded payment moves 5 to B. The exercise later finds that the payment should be reversed.

1. Keep the payment record and append a new record transferring the same 5 from B to A.
2. Give the new record its own ID and point its previous reference to the payment record.

**Check your result.** The history contains both payment and reversal. The final balances return to A 12 and B zero.
