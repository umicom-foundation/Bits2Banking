# Turn one retrieved paragraph into an annotated answer

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A local maker guide says a printed bracket uses two washers. A different paragraph mentions four screws.

1. Select the paragraph that describes the bracket washers and record its source ID as Maker-11.
2. Write a one-sentence answer about washers and place Maker-11 beside that sentence.

**Check your result.** The answer says the bracket needs two washers and links the statement to Maker-11.
