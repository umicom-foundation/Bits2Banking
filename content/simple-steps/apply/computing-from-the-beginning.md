# Write and test a short room-check algorithm

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A meeting room checklist says to close any open window and record how many windows were closed. The practice row has statuses open, closed, open.

1. Write the repeated instruction and the choice using ordinary words. Begin the count at zero and consider each window once.
2. Walk the instruction through the three statuses on paper, changing the recorded status and tally when required.

**Check your result.** All three windows are closed at the end. The tally is two and the window already closed leaves the tally unchanged.
