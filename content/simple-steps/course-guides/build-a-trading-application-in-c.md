# Build a Trading Application in C

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Write order A71 with a three-unit buy quantity and a limit of 155 cents per unit. [Turn an order into a record](../../courses/build-a-trading-application-in-c/lessons/01-turn-an-order-into-a-record.html)
2. Cross out the 160-cent offer. Compare sequence 11 and sequence 12 at the equal 150-cent price. [Find an eligible offer by price and order](../../courses/build-a-trading-application-in-c/lessons/02-find-an-eligible-price.html)
3. Try the lesson’s 140-cent limit against the same offer list. Record whether any offer qualifies. [Find an eligible offer by price and order](../../courses/build-a-trading-application-in-c/lessons/02-find-an-eligible-price.html)

**Check your result.** Sequence 11 wins the 150-cent tie. A 140-cent limit admits no offer.
