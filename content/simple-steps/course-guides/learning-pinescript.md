# Learning Pine Script

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Write down closing values 10, 12, 14 and 16. Find the average of the first three, then of the last three. [A chart and a price series](../../courses/learning-pinescript/lessons/01-a-chart-and-a-price-series.html)
2. In Pine Editor, add the lesson's `ta.sma(close, 3)` indicator and find the plotted line. [A chart and a price series](../../courses/learning-pinescript/lessons/01-a-chart-and-a-price-series.html)
3. Add the confirmed-value example and distinguish the currently open bar from a closed one. [Live bars and confirmed values](../../courses/learning-pinescript/lessons/02-live-bars-and-confirmed-values.html)

**Check your result.** The paper averages are 12 and 14. The confirmed-value script draws its result when the bar closes.
