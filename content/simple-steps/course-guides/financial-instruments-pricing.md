# Financial Instruments Pricing Using C, C++ and Java

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Write the fictional payment amount of 1,050, its currency and its payment date one year after valuation. [Cashflows before formulas](../../courses/financial-instruments-pricing/lessons/01-cashflows-before-formulas.html)
2. Convert 5% to 0.05. Divide the payment by `1 + 0.05` for the lesson’s annual model. [Cashflows before formulas](../../courses/financial-instruments-pricing/lessons/01-cashflows-before-formulas.html)
3. Reprice the 525-unit practice payment at 5% and 10%. Compare the two values at the same payment date. [Cashflows before formulas](../../courses/financial-instruments-pricing/lessons/01-cashflows-before-formulas.html)

**Check your result.** The first value is 1,000. The 525-unit payment is worth 500 at 5% and about 477.27 at 10%.
