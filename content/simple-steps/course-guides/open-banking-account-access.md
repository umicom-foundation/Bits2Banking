# UK Open Banking Account Access

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Draw the account holder, provider and bank on paper. Label the practice consent with its ID and purpose. [Follow Account Access Consent](../../courses/open-banking-account-access/lessons/01-follow-account-access-consent.html)
2. Write down the minimum account fields for that purpose and check them against the consent worksheet. [Request a Minimal Account View](../../courses/open-banking-account-access/lessons/02-request-a-minimal-account-view.html)
3. Run or trace the local C23 consent check with the lesson’s allowed and refused cases. [Check the Trusted Consent in C23](../../courses/open-banking-account-access/lessons/03-check-consent-in-c.html)

**Check your result.** The paper access decision permits only the requested, approved view. The revoked consent case is refused.
