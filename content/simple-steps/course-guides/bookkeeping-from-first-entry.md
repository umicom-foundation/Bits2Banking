# Bookkeeping from Your First Entry

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Record the workshop’s 10,000-cent contribution. Move 2,500 cents from Cash to Equipment for the purchase. [Trace a receipt into accounts](../../courses/bookkeeping-from-first-entry/lessons/01-receipts-and-accounts.html)
2. Post the cash loan in the journal by increasing cash and the amount owed. Keep both lines under one reference. [Record both sides of a transaction](../../courses/bookkeeping-from-first-entry/lessons/02-double-entry-journal-and-ledger.html)
3. Record the lesson’s 500-cent repayment. Subtract it from cash and from the loan payable. [Record both sides of a transaction](../../courses/bookkeeping-from-first-entry/lessons/02-double-entry-journal-and-ledger.html)

**Check your result.** After repayment, Cash has a 10,000-cent debit balance and Loan Payable has a 2,500-cent credit balance.
