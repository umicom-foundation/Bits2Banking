# Learning Accounting and Financial Controls

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Record the fictional 500-unit equipment purchase using a debit to Equipment and a credit to Cash. [Read a balanced journal](../../courses/framework-and-finance/accounting-and-control/lessons/balanced-journals.html)
2. Add both columns and compare them with the original purchase record. Check the date and the accounts. [Read a balanced journal](../../courses/framework-and-finance/accounting-and-control/lessons/balanced-journals.html)
3. Compare the journal to an independent report. Record any mismatched references, currencies or dates as well as totals. [Explain where a report's numbers came from](../../courses/framework-and-finance/accounting-and-control/lessons/reconciliation-and-lineage.html)

**Check your result.** The journal has 500 on each side, and the comparison identifies the original purchase that justifies both lines.
