# Banking Technology Foundations

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Add the posted credit of 600 cents to the opening balance of 3,000. [Read an account, its ledger and its holds](../../courses/banking-technology-foundations/lessons/01-read-a-bank-account.html)
2. Subtract the 900-cent hold to find the amount available. Repeat using a 500-cent hold. [Read an account, its ledger and its holds](../../courses/banking-technology-foundations/lessons/01-read-a-bank-account.html)
3. Compare the two statements for reference P73 and note the difference in amount and currency match. [Reconcile amounts by reference and currency](../../courses/banking-technology-foundations/lessons/03-reconcile-with-stable-identities.html)

**Check your result.** The ledger stays at 3,600 cents. Availability with the smaller hold is 3,100 cents. P73 differs by 50 USD cents.
