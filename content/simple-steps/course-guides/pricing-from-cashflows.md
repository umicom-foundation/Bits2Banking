# Pricing Financial Instruments from Cash Flows

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Draw a timeline and write each practice payment beside its date. [Put payments on a timeline and discount them](../../courses/pricing-from-cashflows/lessons/01-build-a-cashflow-timeline.html)
2. Discount the two sample payments using the supplied annual rate and add the present values. [Put a price on a bond's dated promises](../../courses/pricing-from-cashflows/lessons/02-price-a-fixed-rate-bond.html)
3. Repeat using the lesson’s slightly higher rate. Record the difference and check the direction of the change. [Measure a bond price's rate sensitivity](../../courses/pricing-from-cashflows/lessons/03-measure-an-interest-rate-shift.html)

**Check your result.** The classroom fixed-rate bond example gives about $98.14 at 5 percent. Each amount has a payment date and a discount factor.
