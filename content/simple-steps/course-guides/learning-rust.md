# Learning Rust

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Read `activity.rs` beside `main.rs`. Locate the `Ok` and `Err` results and the caller's matching branches. [Represent success and refusal](../../courses/learning-rust/lessons/03-represent-success-and-refusal.html)
2. Trace the wrapper for inputs 0, 41 and 999. Write one expected output for each. [Represent success and refusal](../../courses/learning-rust/lessons/03-represent-success-and-refusal.html)
3. Trace the inputs 1000 and −1 through the C bridge and the Rust refusal branch. [Represent success and refusal](../../courses/learning-rust/lessons/03-represent-success-and-refusal.html)

**Check your result.** The first three produce counts 1, 42 and 1000. The latter two produce a refusal.
