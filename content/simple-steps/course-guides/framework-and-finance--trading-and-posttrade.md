# Introduction to Trading and Post-Trade Systems

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Write a simulated order asking to buy ten units for at most 25 per unit. Give it an order reference. [An order is a request, a fill is an event](../../lessons/07-practical-domains/framework-studies/trading-and-posttrade/orders-and-executions.md)
2. Record a fill of four units against that order and calculate the open quantity. [An order is a request, a fill is an event](../../lessons/07-practical-domains/framework-studies/trading-and-posttrade/orders-and-executions.md)
3. Mark the remaining quantity as cancelled in the practice record. Keep the completed fill in the history. [Cancel the remainder while preserving completed fills](../../lessons/07-practical-domains/framework-and-finance/trading-and-posttrade/15-cancel-the-unfilled-remainder.md)

**Check your result.** Your final record shows four filled units, six cancelled units and zero still open.
