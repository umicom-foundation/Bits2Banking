# Introduction to Bitcoin

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. On paper, write one unspent output marked A with 20 units. [Payments and shared records](../../lessons/14-blockchain-ledgers/introduction-to-bitcoin/01-payments-and-shared-records.md)
2. Cross out A when the fictional payment is accepted. List the two new outputs, 11 to the recipient and 8 as change. [Payments and shared records](../../lessons/14-blockchain-ledgers/introduction-to-bitcoin/01-payments-and-shared-records.md)
3. Put the payment in block B and draw one block after it. Count B and the later block when finding confirmations. [Confirmations and a reading path](../../lessons/14-blockchain-ledgers/introduction-to-bitcoin/03-confirmations-and-a-reading-path.md)

**Check your result.** The fee is one unit, A is crossed out and the example has two confirmations if both blocks are in the selected chain.
