# FpML for Treasury and Trade Confirmation

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Draw the two legs of the example. Put Cedar on the fixed-paying side and Birch on the floating-paying side. [Read the Two Sides of a Swap](../../courses/fpml-for-treasury/lessons/01-read-the-two-legs.html)
2. Find both leg directions, the currency and the rate information in the practice XML. Copy those fields into a table. [Read and Validate the Import Boundary](../../courses/fpml-for-treasury/lessons/02-read-the-import-boundary.html)
3. Use the C23 check to compare the example with the copy that names Cedar as both payers. [Check the Mapped Terms in C23](../../courses/fpml-for-treasury/lessons/03-check-terms-in-c.html)

**Check your result.** The example accepts `TRN-401` and rejects the altered copy for a leg direction error.
