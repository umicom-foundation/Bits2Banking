# Introduction to Secure Connected Services

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Draw the route for a fictional transfer from the signed-in user to the permission check and the payment service. [Knowing who asked and what they may do](../../lessons/06-umicom/framework-studies/secure-connected-services/identity-and-authority.md)
2. Make a table for the person who prepares the transfer and the person who approves it. Write the action each role may take. [Knowing who asked and what they may do](../../lessons/06-umicom/framework-studies/secure-connected-services/identity-and-authority.md)
3. Record a missing-input observation, a proposed correction, and the result you would check after a reviewer approves it. [Turn an observation into a reviewed change](../../lessons/06-umicom/framework-studies/secure-connected-services/reviewable-automation.md)

**Check your result.** Your page identifies the approver for the fictional transfer and includes separate entries for the proposed change and its expected result.
