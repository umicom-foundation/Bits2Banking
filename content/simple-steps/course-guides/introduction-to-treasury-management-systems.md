# Introduction to Treasury Management Systems

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Set up a cash table with columns for day, money in, money out and available balance. Start Monday at £5,000. [Money, banks and the first question in treasury](../../lessons/09-treasury-tms-calypso/01-what-is-money-and-what-does-a-bank-do.md)
2. Enter Tuesday’s £6,000 payment, Thursday’s expected £1,200 donation and Friday’s £500 bill. Calculate the running balance. [Treasury: making sure payments can be made on time](../../lessons/09-treasury-tms-calypso/02-what-treasury-departments-actually-do.md)
3. Move the donation to the following Monday and calculate Friday’s balance again. Mark the first day with a shortfall. [Treasury: making sure payments can be made on time](../../lessons/09-treasury-tms-calypso/02-what-treasury-departments-actually-do.md)

**Check your result.** Tuesday is the first £1,000 shortfall. Friday’s forecast is −£1,500 when the donation arrives after Friday.
