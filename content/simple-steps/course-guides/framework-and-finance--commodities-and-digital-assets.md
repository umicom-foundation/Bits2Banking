# Introduction to Commodity and Digital-Asset Systems

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Convert an order for two tonnes into kilograms. Keep the delivery details with the quantity. [A quantity needs a unit and a delivery context](../../courses/framework-and-finance/commodities-and-digital-assets/lessons/quantity-and-delivery.html)
2. Try converting 2,501 kilograms into whole tonnes. Write the residual amount before choosing how to represent it. [A quantity needs a unit and a delivery context](../../courses/framework-and-finance/commodities-and-digital-assets/lessons/quantity-and-delivery.html)
3. For the digital-asset example, record the observed transaction, its network and the rule used to decide finality. [A ledger observation is not always final](../../courses/framework-and-finance/commodities-and-digital-assets/lessons/confirmations-and-finality.html)

**Check your result.** You keep the extra 501 kilograms visible and record a rule before calling the ledger observation final.
