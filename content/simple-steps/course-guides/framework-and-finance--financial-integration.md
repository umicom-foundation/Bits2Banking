# Learning Financial-System Integration

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Map the fictional GBP payment from decimal text `12.50` to 1,250 integer pence and record the currency separately. [Translate meaning, not just field names](../../courses/framework-and-finance/financial-integration/lessons/mapping-boundaries.html)
2. Trace maker and checker decisions in the capstone. Record the single balance movement and its matching journal entry. [Trace a controlled payment from request to report](../../courses/framework-and-finance/financial-integration/lessons/lifecycle-capstone.html)
3. Replay the request with its original identifier and compare the result with the independent statement. [Trace a controlled payment from request to report](../../courses/framework-and-finance/financial-integration/lessons/lifecycle-capstone.html)

**Check your result.** The remaining balance is 8,750 pence before fees. The replay causes no further debit and the statement trace identifies one payment.
