# Blockchain Technology and Distributed Ledgers — From First Principles

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Write an opening balance of £100. Enter debit T1 for £30 and credit T2 for £15 in order. [Blockchain Lesson 001 — Understand why people maintain ledgers](../../courses/blockchain-ledgers/lessons/001-understand-why-people-maintain-ledgers.html)
2. Calculate the closing balance. On a practice copy, add T1 a second time and recalculate. [Blockchain Lesson 001 — Understand why people maintain ledgers](../../courses/blockchain-ledgers/lessons/001-understand-why-people-maintain-ledgers.html)
3. Write the check the ledger needs before applying any event that has already been recorded. [Blockchain Lesson 001 — Understand why people maintain ledgers](../../courses/blockchain-ledgers/lessons/001-understand-why-people-maintain-ledgers.html)

**Check your result.** The correct close is £85 and the repeated debit gives £55. Checking the event ID before applying it keeps the close at £85.
