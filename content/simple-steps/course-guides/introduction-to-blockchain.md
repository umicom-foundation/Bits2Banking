# Introduction to Blockchain

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Write two ledger entries on paper. Start at zero, receive 10, then pay 3. Keep the running balance beside each entry. [A ledger and a state](../../lessons/14-blockchain-ledgers/introduction-to-blockchain/01-a-ledger-and-a-state.md)
2. Draw a second practice record that stores the first record’s digest. Write how a reader checks the link. [Linking records with hashes](../../lessons/14-blockchain-ledgers/introduction-to-blockchain/02-linking-records-with-hashes.md)
3. List two things a participant needs before accepting a set of records, such as the applicable rules and who can participate. [Agreement is more than counting](../../lessons/14-blockchain-ledgers/introduction-to-blockchain/03-agreement-is-more-than-counting.md)

**Check your result.** Your ledger ends at 7. The diagram links record two to a digest of record one, and the acceptance notes identify the rules and membership.
