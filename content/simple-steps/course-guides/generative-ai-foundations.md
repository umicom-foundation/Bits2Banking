# Generative AI: Image and Video Training Foundations

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Choose two photos you own. Keep the original files and write down where each came from. [Prepare image-caption pairs with provenance](../../lessons/05-ai/generative-ai-foundations/02-caption-and-image-pairs.md)
2. Give each photo a literal caption describing the subject and what it is doing. [Prepare image-caption pairs with provenance](../../lessons/05-ai/generative-ai-foundations/02-caption-and-image-pairs.md)
3. Make a small copy of each photo using resize with the aspect ratio locked. Compare the copies with the originals and review each caption. [Resize and batch images without silently changing the task](../../lessons/05-ai/generative-ai-foundations/03-resizing-cropping-and-buckets.md)

**Check your result.** Each entry includes a caption and permission note. The resized copies preserve the subjects and proportions.
