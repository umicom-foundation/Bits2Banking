# Introduction to Treasury Management Systems

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Write a fictional forward with GBP 1,000 going out and USD 1,250 coming in. Put both amounts under the agreed settlement date. [A trade creates future obligations](../../lessons/07-practical-domains/framework-studies/treasury-operations/trade-to-settlement.md)
2. Record the earlier trade date on a separate line. Mark each currency obligation as due on the settlement date. [A trade creates future obligations](../../lessons/07-practical-domains/framework-studies/treasury-operations/trade-to-settlement.md)
3. Compare both expected movements with a practice statement that shows only the outgoing GBP payment. [A trade creates future obligations](../../lessons/07-practical-domains/framework-studies/treasury-operations/trade-to-settlement.md)

**Check your result.** Your reconciliation matches the GBP payment and shows USD 1,250 still awaiting evidence.
