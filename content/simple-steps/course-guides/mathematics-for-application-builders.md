# Mathematics for Application Builders

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Group three rows of four seats and record the total with its unit. [Count, group and keep track of units](../../courses/mathematics-for-application-builders/lessons/01-count-in-cents.html)
2. Shade three out of four equal parts, then write the fraction, decimal and percentage beside it. [Describe a part in three ways](../../courses/mathematics-for-application-builders/lessons/02-fractions-decimals-and-percentages.html)
3. Take one missing amount from the equation exercise. Solve it and replace the letter in the original equation to check it. [Find a missing number and prove it](../../courses/mathematics-for-application-builders/lessons/04-solve-an-equation.html)

**Check your result.** Your result shows units, and substituting the missing amount back into the original equation makes both sides equal.
