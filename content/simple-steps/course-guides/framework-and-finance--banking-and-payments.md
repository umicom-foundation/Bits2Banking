# Introduction to Banking and Payments

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

1. Record £12.34 as 1,234 pence and attach the GBP currency label. [Represent money and available funds](../../courses/framework-and-finance/banking-and-payments/lessons/money-and-available-balance.html)
2. Trace the lesson’s £25 reservation. When it posts as a £25 debit, remove the matching hold. [Represent money and available funds](../../courses/framework-and-finance/banking-and-payments/lessons/money-and-available-balance.html)
3. Follow the lesson’s payment instruction and record one piece of evidence for each later state. [Follow a payment from instruction to evidence](../../courses/framework-and-finance/banking-and-payments/lessons/payment-states.html)

**Check your result.** Posting the held £25 reduces the recorded balance by £25 and releases the hold. The amount available does not fall a second time.
