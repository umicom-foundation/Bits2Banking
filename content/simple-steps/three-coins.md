# Write down three coins

![Umicom Foundation](../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Imagine a jar with two coins. You put in one more. How many are there now?

1. Write **Start with 2**.
2. Write **Add 1**.
3. Write **Now there are 3**. Count on your fingers to check.

You have made a record of a change. Bookkeeping uses records to show what came in, what went out, and what is left.

**Try it.** Take one coin out of your pretend jar. What would you write now?

You can write **Take out 1** and **Now there are 2**.
