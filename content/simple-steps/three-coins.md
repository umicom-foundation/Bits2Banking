# Record money in and out

![Umicom Foundation](../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Use a made-up coffee stall. It starts the day with £20 in its till.

1. Write **Opening cash £20**.
2. Record a coffee sale of **£3 in**. The till now has **£23**.
3. Record a purchase of supplies for **£2 out**. The till now has **£21**.
4. Check the closing amount using `20 + 3 - 2 = 21`.

Each entry says what happened, the amount, and whether money came in or went out. Add a date if you plan to keep records for more than one day.
