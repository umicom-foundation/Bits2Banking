# Compare two interval expressions

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A venue charges the weekday rate for hours at least 9 and below 18.

1. Make the input list 8, 9, 17 and 18. Trace both comparisons explicitly before running the examples.
2. Write the same interval in two chosen languages and compare their output tables.

**Check your result.** The accepted inputs are 9 and 17 in both. 8 and 18 are outside the interval.
