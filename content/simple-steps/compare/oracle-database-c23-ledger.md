# Compare representation and displayed precision

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional record stores 9.00 and another 9.05 in exact decimal form.

1. Convert both to integer cents and mark the expected display of two fractional digits.
2. Compare the exact cents with the two display strings and explain why trailing zero belongs in the display of the first row.

**Check your result.** The representations are 900 and 905 cents, displayed as 9.00 and 9.05.
