# Compare an acknowledgement and settlement evidence

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice gateway acknowledges fictional P33 at 09:00. The later settlement record ties P33 to the same currency and amount at 13:00.

1. Write two evidence rows with owner, time and statement meaning. Read Follow an instruction from entry to settlement alongside them.
2. Mark what the application can report at 09:00 and what it can update after reviewing the 13:00 record.

**Check your result.** At 09:00 the trace shows receipt of P33. At 13:00 matching settlement evidence supports a settled state.
