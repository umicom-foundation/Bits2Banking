# Compare a booked balance with a practice available balance

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A sample bank sheet has a booked balance of 700 cents and a hold of 200 cents. The classroom rule subtracts the hold when finding available cash.

1. Copy the booked amount from the ledger row and record the hold on its own line.
2. Apply the available-balance rule and compare both labelled values.

**Check your result.** Booked balance is 700 cents. Available balance under this exercise's rule is 500 cents.
