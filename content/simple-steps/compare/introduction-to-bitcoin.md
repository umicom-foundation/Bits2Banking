# Compare a candidate digest with a classroom work target

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A simplified proof-of-work worksheet says the numerical result must be below 20. It supplies completed results of 11 and 34 for two candidate records.

1. Compare each supplied result with the same target. Mark which candidate meets the worksheet rule.
2. Record the comparison result beside each candidate in the practice log.

**Check your result.** The candidate with result 11 meets the target. The candidate with result 34 does not meet it.
