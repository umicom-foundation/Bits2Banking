# Compare status and settlement receipts

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two synthetic records share payment ID S18. One says accepted and one supplies the course’s settlement evidence.

1. Write the role of each record next to its ID and record its time or order in the exercise.
2. Compare the state you can show after the first record with the state supported by the second.

**Check your result.** Acceptance can be visible before settlement. The matching evidence adds the later settlement state.
