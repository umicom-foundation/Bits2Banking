# Compare immediate and delayed classroom value

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

The same fictional cash flow of £110 is compared today and after one period at a training rate of 10 percent.

1. Record both payment dates. Compute the present value for the delayed payment using the supplied rate.
2. Choose an amount that would grow back to £110 after one period and compare it to your result.

**Check your result.** The immediate amount has a present value of £110. The delayed amount has a classroom present value of £100.
