# Compare a logical and a bitwise operator

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

The C++20 word-operator lesson includes and and bitand. In a classroom expression both receive the integer operands 2 and 1.

1. For 2 and 1, record each operand's truth value and combine them.
2. For 2 bitand 1, write the two low bits 10 and 01 and combine each column independently.

**Check your result.** 2 and 1 is true. 2 bitand 1 is the integer 0.
