# Compare a fraction and a percentage

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A venue has 3 of its 12 seats occupied. A second has 2 of its 8 occupied.

1. Reduce the fractions, then convert each to a decimal and percentage.
2. Write how many empty seats each venue has. Compare how a percentage and raw count answer different questions.

**Check your result.** Both are 25 percent occupied. The empty counts are 9 and 6.
