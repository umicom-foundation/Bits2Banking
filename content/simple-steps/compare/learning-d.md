# Compare ref and out on an initialized integer

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A D worksheet creates two caller integers with initial value 7. One function increments its ref parameter and one increments its out parameter.

1. Trace the parameter value at entry and after the increment for the ref case.
2. Trace initialization at entry and the same increment for the out case.

**Check your result.** The ref parameter starts at 7 and leaves 8 in the caller. The out parameter starts at 0 and leaves 1.
