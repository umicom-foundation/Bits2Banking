# Compare a business date with a timestamp

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

One fictional record provides business date 7 June. Another provides a timestamp at 23:30 UTC on 7 June. The exercise policy calculates business date at a fixed offset of UTC plus two hours.

1. Keep the given business date as a calendar value. In a separate row convert the timestamp using the stated offset.
2. Write each resulting business date and state whether the two observations land on the same date under this policy.

**Check your result.** The supplied date remains 7 June. The timestamp becomes 01:30 on 8 June in the policy's time zone and maps to 8 June.
