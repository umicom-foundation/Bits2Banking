# Compare a count of votes with the agreement rule

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A classroom ledger simulation has four known participants and requires three approvals from distinct participants. One record carries three approvals from one person. Another carries one approval each from three people.

1. Count the approval lines on both records. Then count the distinct participants in each.
2. Apply the classroom rule to the distinct counts.

**Check your result.** Both records have three approval lines. The distinct counts are one and three, so only the second meets the rule.
