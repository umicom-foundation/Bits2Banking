# Compare two stated discount models

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional £105 payment is due in one year. One classroom method uses simple one-year growth of 5 percent. The other uses a continuously compounded rate of 0.05.

1. Use the first course lesson to divide 105 by 1.05. Use the second lesson's expression for the continuous model.
2. Record each model beside its result. Round both to two decimals for display.

**Check your result.** The simple model gives £100.00. The continuous model gives £99.88. Each result carries its own calculation convention.
