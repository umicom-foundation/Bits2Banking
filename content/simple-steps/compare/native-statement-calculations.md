# Compare a delta with the whole posted position

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A known posted opening total is 1,500 cents. Two new posted rows add 100 and −60 cents.

1. Write the signed delta for the two rows, then add it to the known opening amount.
2. Compare the calculated change to the whole result. Keep both values labelled in the practice report.

**Check your result.** The change is 40 cents. The complete posted position is 1,540 cents.
