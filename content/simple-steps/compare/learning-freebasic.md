# Compare a copied value with a caller-owned value

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A scratch variable minutes starts at 10. A procedure adds 2 to its parameter.

1. Trace one call with a ByVal parameter. Reset the caller’s minutes to 10 before tracing the ByRef variant from the procedures lesson.
2. Record the parameter value and the caller’s value at the end of each procedure.

**Check your result.** The procedure parameter ends at 12 in both traces. The caller stays at 10 with ByVal and reaches 12 with ByRef.
