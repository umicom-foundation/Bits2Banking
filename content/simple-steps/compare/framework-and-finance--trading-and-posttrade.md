# Compare reports from two simulator configurations

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two local simulations use the same price fixture. Their starting cash values are 900 and 1,200 practice cents. Each applies an identical 200-cent filled purchase.

1. Record starting cash and the filled amount beside each simulation's own identifier.
2. Compute ending cash separately and compare the two reports.

**Check your result.** The first simulation has 700 cents left. The second has 1,000 cents left. Each result belongs to its original simulator.
