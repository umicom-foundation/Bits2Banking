# Compare an asset contribution and a loan

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two separate fictional workshops each start from an empty ledger. One owner personally buys £40 of equipment and contributes it. The other workshop receives a £40 cash loan.

1. Choose the asset and its matching credit account for each event. Put the journal entries side by side.
2. Calculate assets, liabilities and equity for each workshop after its single event.

**Check your result.** Both workshops show £40 of assets. The contribution has £40 of equity and zero liabilities. The loan has £40 of liabilities and zero equity.
