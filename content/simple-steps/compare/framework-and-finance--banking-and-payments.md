# Compare a balance figure and a spending decision

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional open account and a blocked account both show 500 available minor units. The practice payment policy refuses blocked accounts and still requires checks for open ones.

1. Record the amount and account state in separate fields. Use Separate a balance display from spending authority to identify the owning component for each.
2. Trace a request to spend 100 from each under the stated policy. Note the next decision in the open account case.

**Check your result.** Both views display 500. The blocked request stops at the state rule. The open request can continue to the remaining authorization checks.
