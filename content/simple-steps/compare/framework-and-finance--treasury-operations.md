# Compare gross obligations with a net estimate

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A worksheet has an EUR receivable of 1,200 cents and an EUR payable of 700 cents. The two obligations are with different parties.

1. Keep the receivable and payable on their own lines. Calculate the net cash estimate on a third line.
2. Compare the net line with the two items that remain to be tracked.

**Check your result.** The net estimate is a 500-cent receipt. The gross receivable is 1,200 cents and the gross payable is 700 cents.
