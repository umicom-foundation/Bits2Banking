# Compare a duplicate export with independent evidence

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two copies of a fictional payment export both show 640 cents under source identity A12. A separately produced practice statement also reports that amount for the same reference.

1. Draw three evidence cards and mark which two come from the same original record.
2. Report the numerical agreement and count independent sources. Keep the two export copies under a single origin.

**Check your result.** All three cards show 640 cents. There are two independent source origins: the original export and the independently produced statement.
