# Check progress before division

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice progress helper requires a total greater than zero. Two proposed calls are completed 0 out of 5 and completed 0 out of 0.

1. Use the count-validation lesson to write one case per argument pair. Record whether each pair can reach division.
2. Check the displayed result for the valid pair and the explicit refusal for the zero total.

**Check your result.** Zero out of five reports 0 percent. Zero out of zero reports Total must be greater than zero without dividing.
