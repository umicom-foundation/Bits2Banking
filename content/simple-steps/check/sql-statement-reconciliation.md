# Check a matching and unmatched practice account

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A appears at 300 cents on both sides. B appears at 90 cents on only one side.

1. Trace the course comparison and write one expected status per account. Test the full review shape rather than the matching-only example.
2. Identify which row has a factual difference and which lacks a counterpart.

**Check your result.** A matches. B reports a missing counterpart and is not labelled as a numeric zero.
