# Check that a damaged row cannot replace a good view

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A synthetic Framework account book already produced the course's report of two GBP accounts. Trial A refreshes that valid source. Trial B sets an unrelated account's availability to 501 while its ledger remains 500.

1. Use Preserve the last good balance after failure to trace publication after all records are checked. Keep the last valid output available before each trial.
2. Record the status and whether each action publishes a result. For the refused case compare the saved ledger and available totals.

**Check your result.** Trial A succeeds and publishes ledger 12,000 and available 9,500. Trial B returns INVALID_STATE and keeps the previous report with those totals.
