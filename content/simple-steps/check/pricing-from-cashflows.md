# Check a classroom timeline with two known dates

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Compare £100 paid now and £110 paid in one period using a 10 percent practice rate.

1. Place each payment at its own date. Calculate each present value using the course’s one-period rule.
2. Check both against the same expected present value and preserve the original future amounts alongside them.

**Check your result.** Both have a classroom present value of £100 under the given dates and rate.
