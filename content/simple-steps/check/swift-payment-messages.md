# Check a matching and mismatched status report

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two fictional reports arrive after instruction F2. One refers to F2 and the other refers to F3.

1. Match each report against the instruction’s ID before applying its status. Keep both original messages in your observation records.
2. Check which one can update the visible status of F2.

**Check your result.** The F2 report can update F2 under the exercise’s rule. The F3 report remains unrelated to that instruction.
