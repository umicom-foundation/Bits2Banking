# Check an admitted and refused local image size

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A toy renderer admits images no wider than 640 and no taller than 480. Use 640 by 480 and 641 by 480.

1. Record both dimension checks before running or tracing the local exercise.
2. Compare the resulting readiness observations for the two fixtures before any presentation step.

**Check your result.** The exact-limit image fits. The 641-wide image crosses the width limit and is refused.
