# Check the last day of a cash horizon

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice horizon runs from Monday through Friday, both included. Two isolated sample receipts of 75 cents are dated Friday and the following Saturday.

1. Put the Friday receipt in the forecast and record the inflow within the horizon.
2. Reset the fixture and run the Saturday receipt through the same date filter.

**Check your result.** The Friday fixture contributes 75 cents. The Saturday fixture contributes zero cents to that horizon.
