# Check an orthogonal pair and an aligned pair

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Use `[1, 0]` against `[0, 1]` and `[2, 0]` in the dot-product exercise.

1. Predict which pair shares a contribution in a common position. Multiply matching coordinates in each pair.
2. Compare your two totals with what the vectors represent. Keep the zero contribution visible.

**Check your result.** The first dot product is 0. The second is 2.
