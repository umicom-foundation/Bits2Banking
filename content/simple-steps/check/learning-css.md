# Check what happens to focus styling when focus moves

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice page has two keyboard-focusable links, Home and Lessons. Its focus style gives the focused link a visible outline.

1. Use Tab to focus Home and inspect the focused link's outline.
2. Tab to Lessons and check both links at once.

**Check your result.** The first check shows a focus outline on Home. The second shows the outline on Lessons and clears the focus outline from Home.
