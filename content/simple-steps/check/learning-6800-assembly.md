# Check the zero flag with two small results

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two paper 6800 subtraction traces each start with hexadecimal 07 in A.

1. Subtract hexadecimal 07 in the first trace and label the result and zero flag.
2. Subtract hexadecimal 06 in the second trace and label its result and zero flag.

**Check your result.** The first trace yields hexadecimal 00 with zero set. The second yields hexadecimal 01 with zero clear.
