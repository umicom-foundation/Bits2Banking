# Check both sides of a simulation's cash limit

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A local simulator uses a simple exercise rule that a purchase may consume all available cash. Two isolated trials each begin with 500 practice cents.

1. Apply a purchase costing 500 cents in the first trial and record acceptance and remaining cash.
2. Apply a purchase costing 501 cents in the second trial and record the same values.

**Check your result.** The first trial accepts the purchase and has zero cents left. The second refuses it and retains 500 cents.
