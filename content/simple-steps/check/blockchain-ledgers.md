# Check a nonce before changing a toy balance

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A paper account model starts with next nonce 6 and 12 units. The exercise rules require the next nonce and sufficient balance. Two isolated trials both request five units, with nonce 6 or 5.

1. Start each trial from the original record. Compare its submitted nonce and amount to the model rules.
2. Write the acceptance decision and resulting balance and next nonce in each row.

**Check your result.** Nonce 6 succeeds, leaves 7 units and advances the next nonce to 7. Nonce 5 is refused and leaves 12 units and next nonce 6.
