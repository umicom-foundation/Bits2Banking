# Check that captions follow their own image IDs

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A local experiment fixture contains images M1 and M2. Their source catalogue captions are Desk and Lamp respectively.

1. Give the check a batch with M2 and M1 in that order and the matching captions Lamp, Desk.
2. Keep the same image order in the second batch, but supply the captions Desk, Lamp.

**Check your result.** The first batch has two correctly matched pairs. The second has two mismatches identified by M2 and M1.
