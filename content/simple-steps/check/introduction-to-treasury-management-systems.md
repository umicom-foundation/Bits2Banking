# Check one matching and one unmatched statement entry

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice statement shows receipt R4 for 260 cents and R5 for 300 cents. The journal contains only R4 for 260 cents in the same practice account.

1. Compare R4 against the journal by its ID and verify the two amounts.
2. Look for R5 using the same rule and record its unmatched status separately.

**Check your result.** R4 is matched at 260 cents. R5 stays open for review as an unmatched statement entry.
