# Check the ownership evidence on two paper payments

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A classroom validation sheet represents Alice's authority with a labelled signature card. The first paper payment presents a matching card. The second omits it.

1. Use the worksheet rules to mark whether the first payment supplies the required ownership evidence.
2. Check the second with the same rules and keep the result separate from the question of block inclusion.

**Check your result.** The first satisfies the worksheet's signature requirement. The second has missing signature evidence. Neither result supplies a confirmation count.
