# Verify one equation in both directions

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice balance equation is 5 + 2x = 17.

1. Test the proposed x values 6 and 7 in the original expression. Keep each calculation on its own line.
2. Work backward from 17 by removing the fixed 5 and dividing by 2. Compare that value with your two substitutions.

**Check your result.** 6 gives 17 and 7 gives 19. Solving backward gives 6.
