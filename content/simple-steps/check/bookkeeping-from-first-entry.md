# Check a complete journal against an incomplete one

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional £18 cash purchase of equipment has one proposed journal with an £18 debit and an £18 credit. A second version omits the credit.

1. Name the debit and credit accounts, then total both columns for each proposed journal.
2. Record the expected balance check result for both and mark what the incomplete record is missing.

**Check your result.** The complete entry debits Equipment and credits Cash, each £18, and passes. The incomplete version has £18 debit and £0 credit and is short one £18 Cash credit.
