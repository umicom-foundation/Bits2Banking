# Check a ledger entry against the balance rule

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A classroom ledger starts with 6 credits for A and none for B. The lesson rule allows sending only up to the balance. Use a fresh copy for each trial.

1. Apply a transfer of 6 credits from A to B and record both balances.
2. Try transferring 7 from the original state and check the balances after the rejected trial.

**Check your result.** The first trial ends at A 0 and B 6. The second refuses the transfer and stays at A 6 and B 0.
