# Open the intended copy of a text file

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two personal practice folders named July and August both contain notes.txt. July says water meter 17 and August says water meter 21.

1. Open both folders in the file manager and read the complete path to each notes.txt.
2. Open the August copy by its full path, then compare the text with the July copy.

**Check your result.** August/notes.txt says water meter 21. July/notes.txt still says water meter 17.
