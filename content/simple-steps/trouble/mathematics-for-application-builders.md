# Find a mixed unit in a total

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A venue diary adds 90 minutes and 2 hours and reports 92 minutes.

1. Circle the unit on each number. Convert the two hours into minutes before adding.
2. Write the corrected total beside the earlier observation. Convert the same result to hours and minutes for a second check.

**Check your result.** The sum is 210 minutes, which is 3 hours and 30 minutes.
