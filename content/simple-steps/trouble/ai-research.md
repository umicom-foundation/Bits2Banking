# Spot a busy expert

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Four tokens each go to two different experts. Their routes are A and B, A and C, A and B, and A and D.

1. Draw a column for each expert and put a mark in both assigned columns for every token.
2. Count the marks. Record the per-expert counts beside the total number of dispatches in your experiment notes.

**Check your result.** There are eight dispatches. A has four, B has two, and C and D have one each. A receives half of the work placements.
