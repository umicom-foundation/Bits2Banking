# Wait for a confirmed classroom value

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A synthetic open interval moves from 9 to 11 and closes at 9. A marker is intended for confirmed values at 10 or more.

1. Use the live-bar lesson to label the three readings with their open or closed state before making a comparison.
2. Evaluate the marker rule only on the closed reading. Record what the earlier open reading would have suggested.

**Check your result.** The reading of 11 is an unconfirmed candidate. The confirmed value is 9, so the rule leaves the marker clear.
