# Put a delayed payment on the correct day

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A sample forecast allocates a 600-cent outflow to Tuesday. The practice settlement notice gives Wednesday as the cash date.

1. Find the record by its reference and mark the difference between the original date and the cash date.
2. Move the forecast outflow to Wednesday and recalculate the two daily outflow totals.

**Check your result.** Tuesday shows zero cents for this payment. Wednesday shows a 600-cent outflow linked to the original reference.
