# Find the first broken link in three practice records

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Three paper records carry hash labels H1, H2 and H3. Editing record two changes its label to H2-new, while record three still points to H2.

1. Compare each record's previous label with the current label on its predecessor.
2. Circle the first mismatch and write down the two labels that fail to match.

**Check your result.** The link from record three to record two is the first mismatch. The recorded label is H2 and the current label is H2-new.
