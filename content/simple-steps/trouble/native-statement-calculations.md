# Find why a valid total was replaced

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

The last accepted total is 900 cents. The next local example hits an invalid amount after two valid rows.

1. Sketch when the new sum is being accumulated and when it becomes visible. Record the refusal point.
2. Move the publication point after all rows have been checked in a paper trace. Replay the invalid input.

**Check your result.** The old 900-cent total stays visible when the new set fails. No partial sum becomes the accepted result.
