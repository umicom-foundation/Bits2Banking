# Put a proof sibling on the right side

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

In the course's four-leaf tree, proving inclusion of C produces a path with H(D) and the hash of the A-B subtree P. A learner writes C's next parent in D-C order.

1. Draw A, B, C and D from left to right, then circle the two digests used with C.
2. Write the right-hand parent and root in their correct left-to-right order. Keep each direction beside its digest.

**Check your result.** The right-hand parent Q hashes H(C) then H(D). The root hashes P then Q. D goes to the right of C, and P goes to the left of Q.
