# Find related records on both sides of a split

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two fictional paragraphs came from the same document. One is in the training list and the other is in evaluation.

1. Mark the shared source ID before comparing the individual record IDs. Record the conflict next to the split assignment.
2. Assign splits by source group in a corrected practice copy. Check both arrays again.

**Check your result.** The two related records appear in the same split and there is no overlap for their source group.
