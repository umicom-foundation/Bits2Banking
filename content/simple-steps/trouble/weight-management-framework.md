# Find why a decimal entry was refused

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional draft shows `72.1234` kilograms and the course’s supported field has three fractional digits.

1. Separate the whole and fractional parts. Count the digits and compare them to the declared limit.
2. Test `72.123` in a practice copy with the same field rules. Compare the two outcomes.

**Check your result.** The first has one fractional digit too many and is refused. The second represents 72,123 grams.
