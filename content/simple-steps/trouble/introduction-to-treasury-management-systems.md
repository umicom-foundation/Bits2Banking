# Explain a balance difference between two accounts

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two fictional bank accounts use the same currency. The report shows 9,000 cents and shows a single account name. The underlying rows show 5,000 for account A and 4,000 for account B.

1. Compare the report grouping with the account field on each row.
2. Group by the account field and label the combined figure as a currency total.

**Check your result.** The report shows A at 5,000 cents and B at 4,000 cents. The combined total remains 9,000.
