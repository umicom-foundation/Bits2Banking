# Keep an image with missing permission out of a batch

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice catalogue has three image-caption pairs. One has an empty permission field.

1. Flag the incomplete row and keep its image-caption pair out of this batch.
2. Count the usable pairs and write down which original row needs a permission record.

**Check your result.** Two pairs remain in the batch. The excluded row is named in the review sheet with permission pending.
