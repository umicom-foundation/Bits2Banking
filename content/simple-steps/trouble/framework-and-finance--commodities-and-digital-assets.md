# Find mass lost during rounding

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice intake form converts 3,250 kilograms to 3 whole tonnes, then reports exactly 3,000 kilograms when converted back.

1. Use A quantity needs a unit and a delivery context to write kilograms and tonnes on each side of the calculation.
2. Subtract the round-trip mass from the original and record the equivalent exact decimal tonnage.

**Check your result.** The rounding drops 250 kilograms. The original mass is 3.25 tonnes.
