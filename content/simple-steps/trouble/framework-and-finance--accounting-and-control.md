# Check the source dates behind a difference

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional ledger total is 1,000 units on 30 June, and an independent statement is 970 on 29 June. A colleague enters the 30-unit difference as a fee.

1. Write both dates and reported totals on separate rows. Flag the date mismatch next to the comparison.
2. Record the same-date statement and evidence for the fee as two pieces of evidence still needed.

**Check your result.** The observed difference is 30 units across different dates. The fee stays unverified and no journal adjustment is recorded.
