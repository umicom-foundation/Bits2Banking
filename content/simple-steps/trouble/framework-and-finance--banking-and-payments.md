# Keep separate currencies in a customer balance view

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A synthetic customer holds GBP 700 and USD 400 in two practice accounts. The screen prototype says Total 1100.

1. Use Follow customer, account and currency identities to annotate each displayed amount with its currency.
2. Replace the prototype total with one labelled row per currency and confirm both accounts belong to the selected customer.

**Check your result.** The display has GBP 700 and USD 400 on separate rows. It has no combined amount.
