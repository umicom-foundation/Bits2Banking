# Check the sign of the exponent

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice note shows a present value above £100 for £100 due in one year under a positive 5 percent continuously compounded rate.

1. Open One price in C, C++ and Java. Evaluate -0.05 × 365 / 365 before looking at the amount.
2. Compare the exponent's sign with the one in the note. Calculate 100 × exp(-0.05) and round to two decimals for display.

**Check your result.** The exponent is -0.05. The present value is £95.12 to two decimal places.
