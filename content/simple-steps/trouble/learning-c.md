# Correct an inclusive boundary in a decision

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A C exercise says a parcel weighing up to 10 units is accepted, but a parcel weighing exactly 10 is refused.

1. Run the decision on weights 9, 10 and 11, and write the program's result beside the stated rule.
2. Change the comparison from less than to less than or equal to and run the same three inputs.

**Check your result.** The corrected results accept 9 and 10 and refuse 11.
