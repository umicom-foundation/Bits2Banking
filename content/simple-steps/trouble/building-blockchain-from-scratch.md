# Keep a zero spend out of the balance

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

The teaching balance holds 9 units. A proposed record asks to spend 0 units and is marked accepted on paper.

1. Trace the amount check in Validate before changing state using zero as the amount.
2. Correct the entry in your paper trace and write the final balance beside it.

**Check your result.** The amount == 0 check returns false. The event is refused and the balance stays at 9 units.
