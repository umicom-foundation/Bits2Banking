# Find why a counter resets after rotation

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

The training screen shows a count of 3 before rotation and 0 after the activity is recreated.

1. Compare the recreated view's default with the count that was present before rotation.
2. In the local exercise, save the count across recreation and restore it before displaying the new view.

**Check your result.** After rotating, the recreated screen shows 3. One further tap increases it to 4.
