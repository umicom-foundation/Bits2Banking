# Find a balanced entry in the wrong account

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional £25 equipment purchase was posted as a £25 debit to Supplies and a £25 credit to Cash. The trial balance agrees.

1. Compare the original receipt description with the name of the debited account.
2. Write the debit and credit for the intended journal on a separate line, then total each side.

**Check your result.** The intended entry debits Equipment by £25 and credits Cash by £25. Both columns balance before and after the correction.
