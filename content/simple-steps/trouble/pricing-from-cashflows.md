# Find a rate written in the wrong form

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A classroom discount example uses 5 percent, but a practice calculation uses the number 5 directly.

1. Write 5 percent as a decimal. Compare the two discount divisors for one period.
2. Use the corrected divisor on a fictional £105 payment and check by multiplying back.

**Check your result.** The decimal rate is 0.05, the divisor is 1.05 and the practice value is £100.
