# Check the order before using midpoint search

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A midpoint search of 2, 9, 4 for 4 discards the right-hand element after seeing 9.

1. Look at the adjacent pairs and identify where the ascending order breaks.
2. Find 4 with a linear scan of the original list and write its zero-based position.

**Check your result.** The break is 9 followed by 4. The linear scan finds 4 at index 2.
