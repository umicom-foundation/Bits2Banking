# Catch two legs flowing the same way

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A synthetic adapter maps fixed payer 1 and receiver 2. It sets floating payer 1 and receiver 2 as well.

1. Use the draw-the-agreement lesson to mark the direction of both legs.
2. Compare the input values with the two directional checks in Check the Mapped Terms in C23. Write the values for a corrected floating leg.

**Check your result.** The candidate is refused. The corrected floating leg pays from 2 to 1 and the fixed leg still pays from 1 to 2.
