# Combine two practice contributions

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

One worker reports a mean of 2 from 3 observations. Another reports a mean of 6 from 1 observation.

1. Calculate the total contribution represented by each worker. Add the two contributions and the counts.
2. Divide the contribution total by the count. Compare it with the simple mean of the two worker means.

**Check your result.** The sample-weighted mean is 3. The two worker means average to 4.
