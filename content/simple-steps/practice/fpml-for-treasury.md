# Match a trade field with its unit

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A made-up trade uses a 3-month period and a USD amount of 10,000.

1. Find the example fields for period, amount and currency in the course fixture. Record all three on paper.
2. Copy the field map and change only the currency on one side. Mark where the comparison should report the difference.

**Check your result.** The amount and period still match. The currency mismatch remains a separate observation.
