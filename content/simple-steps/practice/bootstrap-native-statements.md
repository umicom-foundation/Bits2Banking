# Check a report at two widths

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice report has a balance, a filter and a table of two entries.

1. Open the course example at a wide browser width. Draw a three-line list of its reading order.
2. Narrow the browser window. Use Tab to reach the filter and compare the visible order with your list.

**Check your result.** The balance, filter and entries remain reachable. Keyboard focus can reach the filter at both widths.
