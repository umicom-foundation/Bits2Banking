# Keep units attached to a position

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional position lists 3 contracts of 100 units each and an unrelated wallet entry of 12 units.

1. Write down the unit and product for both records. Calculate the contract position in its underlying units.
2. Keep the wallet entry in its own row. Describe what evidence is needed before comparing the amounts.

**Check your result.** The contract position is 300 underlying units. The wallet’s 12 units remain separate.
