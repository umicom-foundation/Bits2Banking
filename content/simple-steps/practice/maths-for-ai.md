# Compare two small vectors

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Use `[2, 1, 3]` and `[4, 0, 2]` in a toy dot-product exercise.

1. Multiply the entries in each matching position. Write down the three products.
2. Add the products. Swap the order of the vectors and check the result again.

**Check your result.** The products are 8, 0 and 6. The dot product is 14 in either vector order.
