# Compare two practice copies of a record

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Both copies record heights 20, 21 and 22. Their records agree at 20 and 21, then use different values at 22.

1. Put the two copies side by side. Mark the last height where their records agree.
2. Write the disputed record separately and identify the additional validation evidence you would need before choosing a copy.

**Check your result.** Height 21 is the last shared record. Both versions of height 22 remain visible for review.
