# Check a statement with the keyboard

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice statement has two posted rows, 1,200 and −200 cents, and one pending row of 900 cents.

1. Open the course’s local statement example. Use Tab to reach the filter, select Posted and count the visible rows.
2. Write the posted total before selecting Pending. Change the filter and check the posted total again.

**Check your result.** Posted shows two rows and totals 1,000 cents. Pending shows one row. Changing the filter leaves the posted total at 1,000 cents.
