# Trace one state change

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Use a practice counter that adds one every time you press its control.

1. In your copy of the course’s practice project, find its stored count and start it at 1. Find the text that reads the count.
2. Trigger the control twice in the course’s simulator. Compare the displayed value with your paper trace.

**Check your result.** The trace and display reach 3 after two accepted changes.
