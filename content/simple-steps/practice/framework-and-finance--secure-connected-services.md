# Compare two permission decisions

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional user may prepare a transfer. A different practice role may approve it.

1. Draw the user, requested action and permitted role in three columns. Fill one row for preparation and one for approval.
2. Record the expected decisions from the permission matrix. Compare them with the example’s audit record format.

**Check your result.** Preparation is allowed for its role. Approval by that same role is refused, and the decision is recorded separately.
