# Link a trade and its report

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional order O5 has fills of 4 and 2 units. The confirmation has its own ID C9.

1. Record the fill quantities and the order ID. Give the confirmation its own row linked to O5.
2. Calculate the filled total and check whether a reference on the confirmation is enough to alter it.

**Check your result.** Filled quantity is 6. The confirmation links to that trade evidence and does not post a third fill.
