# Check the length of a small slice

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A D example uses the integers 5, 10 and 15.

1. Use the lesson’s array example to choose the first two elements. Write down the length and their sum.
2. Move the selection forward by one position. Predict the new length and sum, then check them.

**Check your result.** Both slices have length 2. Their sums are 15 and 25.
