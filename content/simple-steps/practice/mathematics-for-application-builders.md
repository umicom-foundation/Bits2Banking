# Solve an exact charge on paper

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional practice invoice has a fixed charge of £5 plus £3 per item. Its total is £17.

1. Write the equation with a letter for the number of items. Remove the fixed charge from both sides.
2. Divide what remains by 3. Check by substituting the result into the first equation.

**Check your result.** There are 4 items. The final check is 5 + 3 × 4 = 17.
