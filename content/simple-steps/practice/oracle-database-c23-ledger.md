# Keep the sign and scale of a practice amount

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

The lesson’s exact-value exercise supplies 12.30 in the currency unit and an optional adjustment.

1. Write the whole and fractional parts. Convert the amount to cents without rounding away the second decimal place.
2. Compare reports for a present adjustment of zero and an absent adjustment field.

**Check your result.** The amount is 1,230 cents. Zero and absent remain distinct in the two reports.
