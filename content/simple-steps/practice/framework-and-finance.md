# Follow one result through a project

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

An offline practice service publishes a calculated amount and a status to a workspace.

1. Choose the workspace and service tracks from the course. Draw one box for the service and one for the application screen.
2. Write where the amount is calculated and where its message is displayed. Attach one result from a local fixture to your notes.

**Check your result.** The notes identify the owner of the calculation, the screen that displays it and the evidence used to check the result.
