# Check a decision at its limit

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fixture asks whether a reading is at least 10. Use inputs 9, 10 and 11.

1. Trace the three values through the decision pattern in the lesson and mark the branch taken.
2. Change the comparison from inclusive to strict in your copy. Predict the revised results, then check them.

**Check your result.** Only the input 10 changes its branch when you switch from at least 10 to greater than 10.
