# Check a sequence before and after a change

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A sequence in the small exercise contains values 3, 6 and 9.

1. Use the course’s sequence loop to count the entries, then add their values.
2. Add 12 in your copy. Predict the new count and sum before rerunning it.

**Check your result.** The original count and sum are 3 and 18. The second run gives 4 and 30.
