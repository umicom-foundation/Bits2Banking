# Keep display filters separate from totals

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

The synthetic statement contains posted rows of 250 and 350 cents and one pending row of 400 cents.

1. Pass the source snapshot into the course’s view and record the posted total.
2. Use the lesson’s filter to show the pending row. Compare the displayed posted total before and after the filter.

**Check your result.** The pending view shows one row. The snapshot’s posted total remains 600 cents.
