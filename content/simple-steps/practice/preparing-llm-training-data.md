# Split related examples together

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Four toy texts belong to source groups A, A, B and C.

1. Give each source group a split before assigning any of its rows. Write the group and split next to each record.
2. Check your split list for a group that appears on both sides. Move related records together where needed.

**Check your result.** Both records from group A share one split. No source group overlaps training and evaluation.
