# Plan an egg timer in seconds

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A recipe calls for 6 minutes. The timer expects seconds.

1. Write the recipe value, multiply it by 60, and state what the display should show.
2. Change the recipe to 8 minutes. Follow the same three instructions and compare the two numbers.

**Check your result.** Six minutes gives 360 seconds. Eight minutes gives 480 seconds.
