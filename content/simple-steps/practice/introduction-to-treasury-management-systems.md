# Separate trade date and settlement date

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional deal is entered on Monday and settles Wednesday.

1. Write both dates on separate rows. Add one value that identifies the same deal on both rows.
2. Mark which date belongs to the entry action and which date belongs to the expected cash movement.

**Check your result.** Monday is the entry date and Wednesday is the settlement date for the same deal.
