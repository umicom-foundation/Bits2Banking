# Balance a small till

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Opening cash is £80. A sale brings in £12 and supplies cost £5.

1. Make one line for the opening cash and one for each movement. Put the cost in the money-out column.
2. Total money in and money out, then check the number that should remain in the till.

**Check your result.** The closing amount is £87. The movement is £7. Keep the £5 expense as its own entry.
