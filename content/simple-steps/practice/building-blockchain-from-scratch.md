# Find a changed earlier record

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A toy chain has records 1, 2 and 3. Change the text of record 2 in a disposable copy.

1. Record the original identity of record 2 and the previous-record identity stored in record 3.
2. Recalculate the changed record with the course’s local exercise. Compare the new identity to the value recorded in record 3.

**Check your result.** The link from record 3 no longer agrees with the revised record 2. Keep the earlier working copy for comparison.
