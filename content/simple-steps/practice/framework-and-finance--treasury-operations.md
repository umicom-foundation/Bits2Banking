# Reconcile one practice cash position

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Opening cash is 5,000 cents. A posted movement adds 1,000. Another movement of 700 is pending.

1. Write a row for the opening amount and each movement. Label the two states before adding anything.
2. Add the posted amount to opening cash. Show the pending amount on a separate line.

**Check your result.** The posted position is 6,000 cents. The pending line shows 700 cents.
