# Trace a practice payment

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional payment P17 is submitted in the morning. Its acceptance arrives before its settlement notice.

1. Make three rows for submitted, accepted and settled. Place a copy of the same payment ID in each row.
2. Cover the settlement row. Describe the last fact the system can show from the two remaining rows.

**Check your result.** The record shows acceptance and keeps settlement pending until the third piece of evidence is available.
