# Check a practice value using a timeline

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A made-up example pays £121 after two periods. Use a training rate of 10 percent per period.

1. Write the payment at period two. Divide by 1.1 for the first discount and again for the second.
2. Check by growing your result twice at the same training rate.

**Check your result.** The practice value is £100. Growing it by 10 percent twice returns £121.
