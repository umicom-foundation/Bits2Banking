# Follow a tiny expression through a compiler

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Use the expression 8 + 4 * 2 in the course’s parser or its equivalent fixture syntax.

1. Write the numbers and operators in their source order. Circle the operator the grammar groups first.
2. Draw a multiply node under the right side of the add node. Calculate the result and compare it with the expression runner.

**Check your result.** Multiplication produces 8 before the addition. The final result is 16.
