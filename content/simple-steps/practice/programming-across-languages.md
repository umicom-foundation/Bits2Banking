# Keep the boundary test the same

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional hall needs at least 12 seats to meet the booking request.

1. Write the comparison in words. Follow it for counts 11, 12 and 13.
2. Translate it using two of the course’s language examples. Run or trace both with exactly those counts.

**Check your result.** Only 12 and 13 meet the condition in both versions.
