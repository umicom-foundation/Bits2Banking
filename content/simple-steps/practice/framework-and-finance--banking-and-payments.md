# Check two payment states

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice payment is approved in the morning and confirmed unsettled at noon.

1. Create one row for approval and one for the noon outcome. Keep the payment ID visible on both rows.
2. Write the status the workspace should show after each row. Add a separate line for later settlement evidence.

**Check your result.** The noon result still shows an unsettled payment, even though the earlier approval remains recorded.
