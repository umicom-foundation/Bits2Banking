# Choose the first two candidates by score

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A next-token worksheet gives the weights 0.55 for tea, 0.30 for coffee and 0.15 for water.

1. Put the three candidate tokens in descending order by weight.
2. Apply a top-two selection to the ordered result and record which token was left out.

**Check your result.** The remaining candidates are tea followed by coffee. Water was excluded.
