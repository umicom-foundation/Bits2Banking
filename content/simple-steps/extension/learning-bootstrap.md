# Check when two cards share a row

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice catalogue contains two cards that should stack on a narrow screen and sit side by side once the medium breakpoint is reached.

1. Put both cards in one Bootstrap row and give each column col-12 and col-md-6.
2. Open the local page at 500 and 900 CSS pixels and compare the card positions.

**Check your result.** The cards occupy separate rows at 500 pixels. They share one row at 900 pixels, in the same reading order.
