# Reuse a progress function with new counts

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice import has eight records. First four records are accepted, then two more are accepted.

1. Use the course’s progress exercise to call the same Kotlin function with completed counts 4 and 6 while keeping the total at 8.
2. Display the two returned percentages in order and compare them to a hand calculation before adjusting the function.

**Check your result.** The two percentages are 50 and 75. The reported total remains 8.
