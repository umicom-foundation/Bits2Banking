# Bring two cash records to one date

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional cash book shows 5,900 cents, including a 400-cent deposit in transit. The same-day bank statement shows 5,350 and includes a 150-cent charge absent from the cash book.

1. Add the deposit in transit to the statement amount. Separately record the charge against the cash book amount.
2. Compare the adjusted totals and mark which item needs a new cash-book entry.

**Check your result.** Both adjusted totals are 5,750 cents. The cash book needs the 150-cent charge entry, and the deposit is already recorded there.
