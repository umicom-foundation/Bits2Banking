# Count seats in open rooms

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A small programme stores two rooms. Gallery is open with 12 seats and Studio is closed with 8 seats.

1. Use a Pascal record to keep the name, open flag, and seat count together for each room.
2. Visit each record and add the seats only if its open flag is true. Then change the Studio record to open and repeat.

**Check your result.** The total is 12 before Studio opens and 20 after it opens.
