# Apply two valid transfers in order

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A paper ledger starts with 10 credits in A and zero in B. The next entries transfer 4 from A to B and 1 from B back to A.

1. Apply the first entry and write down both balances before moving to the next entry.
2. Apply the second entry to those balances and add the final amounts to check conservation.

**Check your result.** The intermediate balances are A 6 and B 4. The final balances are A 7 and B 3, with a total of 10.
