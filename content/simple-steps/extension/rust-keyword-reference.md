# Give a match two accepted labels and a default

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A training report has states Ready, Waiting and Unexpected.

1. Use the course’s pattern-match example to give the first two states their own result. Write a separate result for the remaining case.
2. Try all three inputs and compare the case labels with your written predictions.

**Check your result.** Ready and Waiting produce their own outcomes. Unexpected reaches the chosen default.
