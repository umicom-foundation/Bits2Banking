# Check that two journal entries balance

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice journal has one entry of 12.30 and one of -12.30.

1. Use the ledger invariant lesson to construct both amounts from text with BigDecimal, and add them without converting to double.
2. Remove the second entry in a scratch case and check the same total again.

**Check your result.** The complete journal sums to 0.00. The one-entry journal sums to 12.30.
