# Show the rows behind a daily report total

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional cash collection report covers 12 June. The input is R1 on 12 June for 120 units, R2 on 12 June for 80 and R3 on 13 June for 70.

1. Use the lineage diagram to write the date selection rule and identify the input records admitted by it.
2. Write the report total with its two record IDs and list the excluded row and the reason beside it.

**Check your result.** The 12 June report totals 200 units from R1 and R2. It excludes R3 because its date is 13 June.
