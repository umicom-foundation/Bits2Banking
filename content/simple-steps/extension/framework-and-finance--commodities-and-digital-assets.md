# Recheck a deposit after its block disappears

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A paper ledger policy requires the deposit's verified inclusion in the current chain plus a verified successor block before making the fictional deposit of 40 units spendable. It first appears in block 18, then a reorganisation removes block 18 before the next observation.

1. Mark the deposit pending after its initial appearance. Compare the replacement history with the block containing that deposit.
2. Write the updated spendable amount and which evidence a reviewer needs before accepting the deposit on the new history.

**Check your result.** Spendable credit from the deposit stays 0. The record retains the observation in displaced block 18 and waits for a new canonical inclusion and the policy threshold.
