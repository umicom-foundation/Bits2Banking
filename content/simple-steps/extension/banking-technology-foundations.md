# Follow two active holds through a release

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional account has a posted balance of 5,000 cents, with Hold A for 1,200 and Hold B for 600. Hold B is then released.

1. Use the available-funds calculation from Read an account, its ledger and its holds. Combine active holds before the release.
2. Remove Hold B from the active total, then calculate the new available amount and write the ledger balance again.

**Check your result.** Availability goes from 3,200 to 3,800 cents. The posted balance is 5,000 cents at both points, and Hold A still reserves 1,200.
