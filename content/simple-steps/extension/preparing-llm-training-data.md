# Make an independent toy evaluation group

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

You have fictional examples from four different documents. Two describe scheduling and two describe room capacities.

1. Assign complete documents to training and evaluation. Write down why your evaluation set checks both topic types.
2. Verify that no original source identifier appears in both sets. Capture the rule and the resulting source lists.

**Check your result.** The selected training and evaluation sets contain both topic types and share no source identifier.
