# Value two future classroom payments separately

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional pair of £100 cashflows is due in 365 and 730 days. The teaching assumption is continuous compounding at a decimal annual rate of 0.05.

1. Use the course formula to calculate the two exponents and their separate present values.
2. Add the unrounded present values. Round the values displayed to two decimals.

**Check your result.** The displayed components are £95.12 and £90.48. Their total rounded from full precision is £185.61.
