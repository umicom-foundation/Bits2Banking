# Calculate change and the fee from a paper payment

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A classroom transaction spends one 1,000-satoshi input. It assigns 700 to the recipient and 250 back as change.

1. Add the two outputs, then subtract their sum from the input.
2. Label the recipient amount, change and fee on the worksheet, and check that they account for the whole input.

**Check your result.** The outputs total 950 satoshis and the fee is 50 satoshis. The three labelled amounts sum to 1,000.
