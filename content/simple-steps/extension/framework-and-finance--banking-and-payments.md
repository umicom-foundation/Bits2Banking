# Post a captured reservation once

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A synthetic account has 7,000 pence posted and one reservation of 900 pence. Then that reservation becomes a posted debit.

1. Calculate availability while the reservation is active. Record the balance and reservation on separate lines.
2. Post the 900-pence debit and clear its associated reservation. Recalculate availability.

**Check your result.** Availability is 6,100 pence before capture. After capture the posted balance is 6,100 and the active reservation is zero. Availability is still 6,100.
