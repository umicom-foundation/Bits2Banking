# Link a delivery term to a learning route

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A fictional commodity receipt says 1,750 kilograms but the practice form accepts only whole tonnes.

1. Start at the financial-systems route in Start here and find the course about commodities and digital assets.
2. Open A quantity needs a unit and a delivery context and apply its kilogram-to-tonne conversion.

**Check your result.** The route reaches Introduction to Commodity and Digital-Asset Systems. The exact quantity is 1.75 tonnes, which the whole-tonne field cannot represent.
