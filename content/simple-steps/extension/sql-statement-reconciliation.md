# Add a known mismatch to a joined report

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

Two fictional account summaries initially agree at 500 and 900 cents.

1. Run the comparison pattern on the equal summaries. Change the second amount on one side to 910 in a disposable copy.
2. Repeat the comparison and confirm the first account’s row is kept separate from the second.

**Check your result.** The unchanged account still matches. The second has a difference of 10 cents in the chosen direction.
