# Measure the uncollateralised part of a practice exposure

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A classroom record has an exposure of 900 cents and eligible collateral of 650 cents. This model counts all 650 cents toward the exposure.

1. Subtract the eligible collateral from the exposure and record the result as an uncovered amount.
2. Add another 100 cents of eligible collateral in the worksheet and repeat the calculation.

**Check your result.** The uncovered amount falls from 250 cents to 150 cents.
