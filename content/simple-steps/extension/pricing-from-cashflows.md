# Compare two one-period classroom payments

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A hypothetical exercise has a £100 payment and a £110 payment, both due in one period at a 10 percent training rate.

1. Discount both payments using the same divisor. Write each result with its currency and date convention.
2. Subtract the results and compare the difference to discounting the £10 future difference directly.

**Check your result.** The practice values are about £90.91 and £100. Their difference is about £9.09.
