# Watch a loop total change in the debugger

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A short C program starts at zero and adds 1, 2, and 3 over three iterations.

1. Use the debug lesson to pause at the source line that adds the next value. Continue until the pending addend is 3.
2. Read the total in the debugger before stepping over the addition. Step once and record the total again.

**Check your result.** The total is 3 before the third addition and 6 immediately afterward.
