# Give a route record its own distance method

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice route has two stretches of 6 and 4 kilometres. Both need to travel together as one value.

1. Declare a Route struct with two integer fields and instantiate it with the practice distances.
2. Add a Kilometres method that sums the fields and print the returned value.

**Check your result.** The printed route distance is 10 kilometres.
