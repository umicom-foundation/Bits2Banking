# Stop a payment when approval is denied

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A made-up payment P55 requests 650 pence from a starting balance of 4,000 pence. The request is valid, but the checker denies approval.

1. Follow the lifecycle capstone in order. Record the successful validation and the checker decision using distinct fields.
2. Write the ledger balance and number of new posted journal entries after the decision. Link the refusal record back to P55.

**Check your result.** P55 ends at denied approval. The balance stays at 4,000 pence and the ledger has zero new posted entries for it.
