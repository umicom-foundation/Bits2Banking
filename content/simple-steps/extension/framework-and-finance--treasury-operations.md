# Keep a valuation apart from a cash movement

![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-light width="215" }
![Umicom Foundation](../../assets/branding/umicom-wordmark-on-dark.svg){ .umicom-wordmark .umicom-wordmark-dark width="215" }

A practice forecast starts with 1,000 cents. A valuation increases by 90 cents and a separate pending payment of 120 cents then settles.

1. Write the valuation change in its own column and record the cash effect of a settled payment as a separate movement.
2. Calculate the closing cash using the movement column and report the valuation change on its own line.

**Check your result.** Closing cash stands at 880 cents. The separate valuation line shows an increase of 90 cents.
