# Bits2Banking — System Administration Course Roadmap After Update 40

The *System Administration: From Zero* course is now planned as progressive stages, with actual completion distinguished from proposed work:

| Stage | Chapters | Status / learning outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Starting from zero | 1–24 | Previously delivered in Update 38; terminal, files, basic administration, Git and native development |
| 2 — Intermediate foundations | 25–36 | Previously delivered in Update 39; permissions, services, updates, SSH, networking, storage and verified backups |
| 3 — Practical infrastructure | 37–48 | **Delivered in Update 40**; safe lab, baselines, schedule inspection, volume mapping, ACL observations, network/firewall checks, health and incident investigation |
| 4 — Managed services and recovery | 49–60 (proposed) | Service profiles, task owners, packaging and configuration, monitoring design, disaster recovery drills and portable evidence |
| 5 — Platform administration capstones | 61–72 (proposed) | Build and release operations, training VMs, data protection, reproducible Linux/Windows environments and reviewed Umicom OS service deployment |

Every advanced lesson must remain readable by a diligent absolute beginner who has followed earlier chapters. Begin with a real task, explain new words, use one command at a time with expected results, compare Windows/PowerShell and Linux, introduce relevant risks and finish with assessment. Security-sensitive operations require a supervised isolated lab and explicit approvals; never require access to production credentials or finance systems.

Native reusable tools must be investigated in Umicom Framework before any new implementation. C23 and Assembly are default. New Python/PowerShell automation scripts are prohibited; shell commands remain appropriate as interactive instructions.
