**Bibliographic source:** Ethereum Foundation community documentation

## Relevance to the educational course

## Source interpretation

Not all proof-of-stake systems have Ethereum finality rules. The course's toy calculations, C23 code and diagrams are independent original teaching material. Bibliographic linking does not imply source-code copying, redistribution rights for third-party books or approval to use any training data.

Cryptographic commitments, permissioned ledger governance, federated-learning update admission and model-quality evaluation.
