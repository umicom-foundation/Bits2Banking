# NISTIR 8202 — Blockchain Technology Overview

**Bibliographic source:** Dylan Yaga, Peter Mell, Nik Roby and Karen Scarfone; NIST, 2018  
**Primary reference:** https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8202/final

## Relevance to the educational course

Taxonomy of blockchain, consensus, forks, hashing, public/private distributed ledgers and smart contracts.

## Source interpretation

Source establishes concepts, not an Umicom blockchain deployment. The course's toy calculations, C23 code and diagrams are independent original teaching material. Bibliographic linking does not imply source-code copying, redistribution rights for third-party books or approval to use any training data.

## Related learning areas

Cryptographic commitments, permissioned ledger governance, federated-learning update admission and model-quality evaluation.
