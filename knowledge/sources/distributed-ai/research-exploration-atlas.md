## Architectural distinctions

In the native Framework, *controller and message scheduling* remain separate from the *training tape*. The new teaching round aggregates synthetic or externally trained **weight-space deltas**; it does not solve transport, authentication, dataset authorisation, held-out evaluation or a proof-of-learning consensus problem. When broad research reports describe these prospective components, treat them as design hypotheses until code and independent tests establish a narrower statement.

## Progressive practical studies

1. Produce and inspect a pinned model checkpoint and authorised toy training data.
2. Train or simulate three compatible workers and independently verify weighted FedAvg arithmetic.
3. Inject duplicate, stale, non-finite and oversized updates; inspect failure atomicity.
4. Compare model candidate quality with an isolated centrally trained reference.
5. Vary data partitions and network conditions without changing the declared evaluation target.
6. Only after a complete scientific baseline, study cryptographic receipts, secure aggregation and optional multi-party ledgers.
