# DiLoCo — Distributed Low-Communication Training

**Bibliographic source:** Douillard et al., preprint 2023  
**Primary reference:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08105

## Relevance to the educational course

Periodic outer updates allow many local steps between network synchronisations.

## Source interpretation

Compare experimental methods and compute budgets before claiming a new speedup. The course's toy calculations, C23 code and diagrams are independent original teaching material. Bibliographic linking does not imply source-code copying, redistribution rights for third-party books or approval to use any training data.

## Related learning areas

Cryptographic commitments, permissioned ledger governance, federated-learning update admission and model-quality evaluation.
