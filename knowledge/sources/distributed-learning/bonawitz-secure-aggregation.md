# Practical Secure Aggregation for Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning

**Bibliographic source:** Bonawitz et al., 2017  
**Primary reference:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.04482

## Relevance to the educational course

Secure aggregation studies privacy of individual updates during collective summation.

## Source interpretation

Privacy of updates and robustness against poisoning are distinct problems. The course's toy calculations, C23 code and diagrams are independent original teaching material. Bibliographic linking does not imply source-code copying, redistribution rights for third-party books or approval to use any training data.

## Related learning areas

Cryptographic commitments, permissioned ledger governance, federated-learning update admission and model-quality evaluation.
